Welfare Cleaning for Construction Sites

Clean welfare facilities are a legal requirement under CDM 2015 regulations — not an optional extra. Principal contractors and site managers have a duty to ensure that welfare provisions are maintained to an appropriate standard throughout the life of a project. Failing an audit can halt work, attract enforcement action, and damage your relationship with clients.

Signature Cleans provides professional welfare and construction site cleaning across Devon and the South West. We work directly with major contractors including Bouygues UK, keeping facilities compliant, hygienic, and presentable from groundworks through to handover.

Why Welfare Cleaning Matters

  • CDM compliance – Meeting your legal obligation to provide clean welfare facilities
  • Audit readiness – Pass principal contractor and client welfare audits
  • Workforce morale – Workers who feel looked after are more productive and loyal
  • Reputation – Clean sites reflect well on your management standards
