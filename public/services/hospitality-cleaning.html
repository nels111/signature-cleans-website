Professional Hospitality Cleaning Services

In hospitality, first impressions are everything. A spotless dining room, gleaming bar, and immaculate washrooms are not just nice to have — they are fundamental to your reputation, your reviews, and your repeat business. One bad experience can mean a lost customer forever.

Signature Cleans provides dedicated cleaning services for restaurants, hotels, and venues across Exeter and Devon. We work around your service hours to ensure your space is guest-ready before every opening, without disrupting your operations.

Why Hospitality Cleaning is Different

  • Guest expectations – Customers notice every detail, from table tops to skirting boards
  • Food hygiene – Meeting EHO standards in food preparation and dining areas
  • Reviews and reputation – Cleanliness is the number one factor in guest reviews
  • Turnaround speed – Venues need to be cleaned and ready between events

What's Included

  • Dining area and table cleaning
  • Bar area sanitisation
  • Kitchen cleaning (front and back of house)
  • Guest washroom deep cleaning
  • Lobby and entrance maintenance
  • Conference and function room turnarounds
  • Hard floor cleaning and polishing
  • Outdoor seating and terrace areas
  • All cleaning materials and equipment provided

Flexible Around Your Hours

We understand hospitality does not work a 9-to-5. Our teams can clean early mornings before service, late evenings after close, or during off-peak hours. For events and functions, we provide rapid turnaround cleaning to get your space ready for the next booking.

Who We Work With

  • Restaurants and bistros – Fine dining to casual eateries
  • Hotels and B&Bs – Guest rooms, public areas, and back of house
  • Pubs and bars – High-traffic venues requiring daily attention
  • Event venues – Conference centres, wedding venues, exhibition spaces
  • Theatres and cinemas – Auditoriums, foyers, and hospitality areas
