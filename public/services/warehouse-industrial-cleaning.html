Professional Industrial Cleaning Services

Warehouses, showrooms, and industrial facilities present unique cleaning challenges that standard commercial cleaners are not equipped to handle. Large floor areas, heavy soiling, specialist surfaces, and health and safety considerations all require a team with the right equipment, training, and experience.

Signature Cleans provides dedicated cleaning programmes for industrial and large-scale facilities across Devon and the South West. We work with businesses including Porsche showrooms, distribution centres, and manufacturing facilities to maintain environments that are safe, presentable, and operationally efficient.

Why Industrial Cleaning is Different

  • Scale – Large floor areas require industrial equipment and efficient methods
  • Health and safety – Compliance with workplace safety standards and risk assessments
  • Presentation – Showrooms and customer-facing areas demand premium standards
  • Operational continuity – Cleaning scheduled to avoid disrupting your operations

What's Included

  • Warehouse floor sweeping and scrubbing
  • Loading bay and dock cleaning
  • Office areas within industrial buildings
  • Showroom floor polishing and maintenance
  • Workshop and production area cleaning
  • Communal areas and break rooms
  • Washroom and welfare facility cleaning
  • External yard and entrance maintenance
  • All specialist equipment and materials provided

How We Work

We begin with a comprehensive site visit to assess the scale, layout, and specific requirements of your facility. We then build a cleaning programme that addresses your priorities — whether that is pristine showroom floors, clean and safe warehouse walkways, or well-maintained office areas within your building.

Our teams use industrial-grade equipment suited to large spaces, including ride-on scrubber dryers, high-reach dusting systems, and specialist floor treatments. Everything is scheduled around your operational hours to minimise disruption.

Who We Work With

  • Warehouses – Storage, logistics, and fulfilment centres
  • Distribution centres – High-throughput facilities with strict cleanliness standards
  • Car showrooms – Including Porsche, maintaining premium brand presentation
  • Workshops – Engineering, automotive, and manufacturing environments
  • Factories – Production facilities requiring regular maintenance cleaning
  • Industrial estates – Multi-unit sites with shared areas
