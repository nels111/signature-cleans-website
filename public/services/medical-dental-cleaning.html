Specialist Clinical Cleaning Services

Healthcare environments demand a cleaning standard that goes far beyond what a typical commercial space requires. Cross-contamination risks, infection control protocols, and CQC compliance all need to be addressed by a team that understands clinical cleaning — not just general office cleaning applied to a medical setting.

Signature Cleans provides dedicated cleaning programmes for medical and dental practices across Exeter and Devon. Our teams are trained in infection prevention and control procedures, use clinical-grade products, and follow documented specifications designed for healthcare environments.

Why Clinical Cleaning is Different

  • Infection control – Preventing cross-contamination between clinical and non-clinical areas
  • CQC compliance – Meeting the cleanliness standards inspectors expect
  • Patient confidence – A visibly clean practice reassures patients and builds trust
  • Staff safety – Proper handling and disposal of clinical waste and sharps

What's Included

  • Waiting room and reception cleaning
  • Treatment room sanitisation
  • Clinical surface decontamination
  • Bathroom and washroom deep cleaning
  • Hard floor cleaning and disinfection
  • Touch-point sanitisation (handles, switches, rails)
  • Waste segregation and removal
  • All clinical-grade cleaning products provided

How We Work

We begin with a detailed site visit to understand your practice layout, clinical areas, and specific requirements. We then develop a cleaning specification that addresses infection control protocols, cleaning schedules around surgery hours, and any compliance requirements specific to your practice.

Your dedicated team is trained in healthcare cleaning standards and follows documented procedures at every visit. Regular quality audits ensure standards are maintained and give you the evidence you need for CQC inspections.

Who We Work With

  • Dental practices – General, cosmetic, and specialist dentistry
  • GP surgeries – Single and multi-partner practices
  • Private clinics – Dermatology, aesthetics, and specialist clinics
  • Physiotherapy clinics – Treatment rooms and rehabilitation areas
  • Veterinary practices – Clinical and consulting areas
